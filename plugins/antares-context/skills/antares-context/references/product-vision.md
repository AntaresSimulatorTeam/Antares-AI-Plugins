# Product direction: public information and working hypotheses

This note separates published product descriptions from working hypotheses. It is not a formally approved
common vision, roadmap, commitment, or statement of stakeholder consensus. Confirm current objectives with the
relevant product owners and published documentation before using it for decisions.

## Published purposes

- [Antares Simulator](https://github.com/AntaresSimulatorTeam/Antares_Simulator) is an open-source power system
  simulator intended to help assess adequacy and economic performance across time horizons.
- [Antares Web](https://github.com/AntaresSimulatorTeam/AntaREST) offers a web interface and API for managing
  and editing studies. [Antares Craft](https://github.com/AntaresSimulatorTeam/antares_craft) supports
  programmatic access to studies and results.
- The [GEMS project](https://github.com/AntaresSimulatorTeam/GEMS) describes an ambition to support a community
  sharing energy-system models, assumptions and studies. Its published goals include versatility, separation
  of model definition from resolution, and interoperability.
- The [GEMS architecture documentation](https://gems-energy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/overview/architecture/)
  describes model and system definitions in external YAML files. This is a modelling approach, not a guarantee
  that every capability is available in every product or version.

## Working hypotheses for discussion

The following are perspectives for exploration, not validated strategy or official performance measures:

- Useful outcomes may include comparing scenarios, making modelling capabilities accessible through suitable
  interfaces, and reducing the effort needed to express new models.
- A modelling capability's value may depend on more than technical delivery: availability across the relevant
  tools, user qualification and actual use in a study can be distinct steps.
- An interface or performance improvement can reach users differently from a new modelling capability. Confirm
  adoption practices, constraints and evidence with the users concerned; do not assume a universal study cycle.
- Potential measures to discuss include time to obtain results, the ability to explore scenarios, confidence in
  results and interoperability. These are examples, not agreed KPIs or targets.

For cross-product dependencies and version checks, see [interactions.md](interactions.md). For current direction,
seek confirmation from the responsible teams; do not infer a delivery date or promise.
